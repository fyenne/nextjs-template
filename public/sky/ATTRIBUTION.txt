Star positions and constellation linework
-----------------------------------------
The star catalog, constellation lines, and English constellation names in this
directory are from d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn:

  https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial

They are redistributed under the BSD 3-Clause License. See
LICENSE.d3-celestial.txt for the copyright notice, conditions, and disclaimer.

The sky projection, Milky Way dust field, and presentation code are original to
this project.

Moon color map
--------------
public/sky/lroc_color_2k.jpg is the 2048x1024 color map from NASA Scientific
Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit, ID 4720:

  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. Visualizer: Ernie Wright (STC).
The map is painted on a smooth sphere. It is not a scientific product.
